## Final poem (exact text)
```
Two hands on one lever, neither pulling.
Then your thumb eased off, and mine
felt the whole weight it had been fighting.
The gate swung. Whose push, I couldn't say,
only that my hand stayed on.
```

## Revision 2
Scores before the edit (out of 10): agency 8, moves 6, insight 7, craft 7, freshness 6. The weakest element was the ending: "the wanting stayed mine" named the insight as an abstraction instead of showing it.

Change (last two lines only; the rest is untouched):
- Before: `The gate swung. I couldn't say whose push it was, / only that the wanting stayed mine.` (205 chars)
- After: `The gate swung. Whose push, I couldn't say, / only that my hand stayed on.` (193 chars)

Why: "my hand stayed on" is a physical image. Staying on the lever is the choice, so agency is shown rather than asserted, and the poem ends on a small action. It also cuts 12 characters ("it was", "the wanting", "mine") and keeps the blurred-authorship idea.

Not changed: the "neither pulling" vs "fighting" tension in lines 1 and 3. I judged it acceptable (no one pulls, the weight still resists) and chose not to rewrite beyond the single weakest element.
